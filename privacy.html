Privacy policy

ZICA Pune Portal · ZICA Pune, Kothrud · Last updated 27 October 2026

The ZICA Pune Portal is used by enrolled students of ZICA Pune (Kothrud) to book and take software exams and submit assignments. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name and email address, only to match you with your ZICA student record. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.
  • Exam activity: bookings, answers, scores, timings, and exam-integrity events such as leaving the exam tab.
  • Camera snapshots during exams: small still photos taken at the start, at random moments, when an exam rule is broken, and at the end, so the exam can be verified.
  • Assignments: the links you submit and the feedback you receive.

How we use it

Only to run exams and assignments, issue results and certificates, and keep academic records for ZICA Pune. We never sell or share your data with advertisers.

Who can see it

The Academic Head and authorised ZICA Pune faculty. Data is stored securely with Supabase and in ZICA Pune's Google account.

How long we keep it

Results are kept as part of your academic record. Camera snapshots are deleted after your course certificate is issued.

Your choices

You can ask to see or correct your information at any time. Contact the Academic Head at academics.zicapune@gmail.com.
